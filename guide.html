Camper skill guide

Pack for the camper you are today.

A practical guide for first-time campers, relaxed weekend groups, self-sufficient planners, and experienced campers preparing for rougher ground.

View packing levels Overnight permission

What to pack

Choose your comfort level.

Start lighter when facilities are nearby. Add tools as the campsite becomes more remote, wet, or self-managed.

01

Beginner camper

Best for resort stays, family parks, short beach outings, or guided Temburong trips.

  • Tent or booked shelter
  • Sleeping mat, light blanket, pillow
  • Water bottle, snacks, simple meals
  • Torch, power bank, insect repellent
  • Basic first-aid kit and rubbish bags
02

Weekend camper

Good for repeat campers who want better cooking, shade, lighting, and rain comfort.

  • Groundsheet and extra tarp
  • Camp stove, lighter, cookware
  • Cooler box and food storage
  • Lanterns and spare batteries
  • Dry bags for clothes and electronics
03

Group planner

Useful for families, friends, school groups, and office retreats where comfort and backup plans matter.

  • Shared canopy, tables, chairs
  • Meal plan and labelled drinking water
  • Group first-aid and emergency contacts
  • Toilet tissue, hand wash, wet wipes
  • Permit, booking, and transport checklist
04

Expert camper

For remote areas, forest trails, heavy rain planning, and campers who are comfortable solving problems outdoors.

  • Navigation, offline maps, compass
  • Water filter or purification tablets
  • Repair kit, cordage, multi-tool
  • Weatherproof layers and spare footwear
  • Satellite, radio, or agreed check-in plan

Campsite matching

Make the trip friendlier for everyone.

Beginners should look for short travel time, toilets, easy parking, and a clear source or booking contact.

Families benefit from shade, flat ground, nearby water points, and simple food prep instead of complicated camp cooking.

Experienced campers can prioritize quieter forest or water leads, but should confirm access rules, weather, and exit plans first.

Overnight camping permission

Check approval before you camp overnight.

Government-managed recreational forest parks generally require advance permission from the Forestry Department.

How to apply

Contact the Forestry Department first to check campsite availability.

Tel: 2381687 or 2381013

Submit a written application letter addressed to:

Director of Forestry
Forestry Department
Level 2, Ministry of Primary Resources and Tourism
Bandar Seri Begawan BB3910
Brunei Darussalam

Include in the letter

  • Name of applicant
  • Contact number and address
  • Proposed camping date and time
  • Location or campsite requested
  • Purpose of camping
  • Number of participants

Important notes

Camping is only permitted at designated camping areas, such as camping grounds at Berakas Forest Recreation Park.

If entering a Forest Reserve outside public recreational areas, separate approval may be required. Unauthorized entry into Forest Reserves is an offence under the Forest Act.
